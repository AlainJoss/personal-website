# About — personal version

I’m a data scientist and ML engineer based in Zürich. I’m deeply curious about systems and like moving between disciplines. I’m constantly learning new stuff and particularly enjoy building things.

My work spans data pipelines, scientific experiments and simulations, machine learning, and interactive data analysis.

Since starting my master’s, I’ve focused on becoming a generalist across the ML stack.

Between 2021 and 2024, I focused on translating my traditional software engineering knowledge into something usable in the data science domain. I learned about protocols, abstract classes, ... and made my first attempts with interactive data apps.

In 2025, I put considerable effort into making my research approaches more reproducible and interactive, migrating from Jupyter and Streamlit to marimo, pandas to Polars, and Matplotlib to Altair.

In 2026, as the agentic revolution kicked in, I started focusing on developing the right abstractions to automate parts of the stack with reusable building blocks.

In the future I want to turn these automation efforts into tools for scientists and anyone working with data. As LLMs increasingly become part of our workflows, understanding becomes the new bottleneck. My goal will therefore be to enable people not only to retain understanding, but also to develop new mental models, the reason to do science in the first place.
